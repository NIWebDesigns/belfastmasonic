The Consumer Council

Every week the Consumer Council contact home heating oil suppliers from across Northern Ireland to bring you the cheapest, dearest and average home heating oil prices. Consumers can use this information as a benchmark to see how much the average cost of oil is in their region.

www.consumercouncil.org.uk/energy/home-heating-oil

Cheapest Heating Oil

Provides accurate prices for a large number of suppliers based on your postcode

https://www.cheapestoil.co.uk/Heating-Oil-NI

The Energy Saving Trust

The Energy Saving Trust provide energy saving tips to help reduce your energy bills and keep warm.

https://energysavingtrust.org.uk/?loc=northern-ireland

NI Direct

NI Direct is the official Government website for Northern Ireland citizens, providing a single point of access to public sector information and services. They have also launched an on-line benefit calculator which can be found at www.nidirect.gov.uk/benefits-adviser. The service is free and anonymous and can be accessed 24 hours a day.

www.nidirect.gov.uk

Power to Switch

The Power to Switch website offers a free, impartial and independent service to help you find a better energy deal.

www.powertoswitch.co.uk
