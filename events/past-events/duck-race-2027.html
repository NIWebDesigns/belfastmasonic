Duck Race 2025

Quacking Good Fun at the B.M.C.F. Charity Duck Race!

June 21, 2025 — 1:00 PM, Six Mile Water Ballyclare.

The Setting Up Team

The banks of the Six Mile Water were lined with eager spectators yesterday afternoon as some 2,500 bright yellow rubber ducks launched into the river for a truly egg-cellent cause — raising funds for the Belfast Masonic Charity Fund.

The spectators had come from far and wide, including such exotic locations as Ards and Ballymena — but by far our furthest travelled was Most Ex Companion Dimitris Mamas, and his wife who had travelled all the way from Greece especially for the Duck Race (by a lucky coincidence, the Tercentenary of Grand Lodge celebrations are happening at the same time, and he hopes to take part in that when he is here!)

Dimitris Mamas & Mrs Mamas

At precisely 1:00 PM, the flotilla of ducks was released, to a chorus of cheers, laughter, and just a hint of chaos. Adding to the day's festive spirit our main Drake, Paul Williamson, with his fluffy deputy Gary Bond, both in full-blown duck regalia were assisted by a quacking team of experts, Tommy Smyth, Stevie Stanley, Dessie McMullan to guiding the duckies to the finish line.

Paul Williamson Being Chased Down the River By Thousands af Angry Ducks

Desy McMullan & Gary Bond at the Finish Line

Though our civil engineers had constructed a netting collection damn at the finish line, a number of the more adventurous ducks had other ideas. In what could only be described as a feathery mutiny, a large squad of ducks made a successful break for freedom avoiding the net — resulting in a wet and wild retrieval mission that saw a few of the volunteers, including a more unsteady senior brother, getting thoroughly soaked. Talk about getting their ducks in a row! However, nobody quacked under pressure.

Some of the Ducks Attempting a Breakout

And the Winners were;

1st Prize — A dazzling 50-inch smart TV — John Arnott Taylor Lodge 314

2nd Prize — A relaxing weekend break in Donegal — Brother Brian Hamilton, Lodge 831

3rd Prize — £100 cash — Friendly Brothers Lodge 609

John Arnott Taylor Lodge No 314's Duck Won, In What Was Not Quite a Photofinish

Paul Williamson Didn't Get the Memo Saying Smart Casual

Tommy Smith & Jimmy Morrow - The Money Men

After all the excitement (and drying off), everyone made their way back to the garden of the Belfast Masonic Charity Fund building, where a sizzling BBQ awaited. Burgers were flipped, stories were shared, and there may have been a few more duck puns than strictly necessary.

It was a day full of laughs, community spirit, and a wonderful reminder of the good that can come from a bit of fun and a whole lot of rubber ducks. All proceeds will go to supporting the vital work of the Belfast Masonic Charity Fund.

Many thanks to all for your continued support of our fundraising activities.

Until next year — keep calm and quack on!
